# Telecom_Churn_Analysis
This is a data science project for Telecom Churn Predictive modelling

### Why is Churn Analysis necessary?
Customer churn is a critical metric for telecom companies, directly impacting revenue and growth. Understanding churn patterns enables businesses to:

- **Identify at-risk customers** before they leave
- **Reduce customer acquisition costs** by improving retention
- **Increase lifetime value** through targeted retention strategies
- **Optimize resource allocation** for customer support and marketing
- **Gain competitive advantage** in a saturated market
- **Improve profitability** by reducing churn rates and associated losses

By analyzing churn drivers, companies can develop proactive interventions and create better customer experiences.
